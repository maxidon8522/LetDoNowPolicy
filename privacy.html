Child Safety Standards

Let's Do Now is committed to protecting minors and ensuring a safe environment for all users.

We strictly prohibit any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This includes:

  • Sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming or inappropriate communication targeting minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing exploitative content

To ensure user safety, the app provides:

  • In-app reporting system for harmful content or behavior
  • Blocking functionality
  • Content moderation and review processes

Reports are reviewed promptly. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent banning.

We comply with all applicable child protection laws and cooperate with law enforcement authorities when required.

Let's Do Now is designed as a mutual-connection-based social app. Users can only interact with approved connections, reducing the risk of unwanted contact.
