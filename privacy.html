Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Let's Do Now developed by Makishi Murakami.

Information We Collect

The app may collect the following information:

  • Email address (for account authentication)
  • User profile information
  • App activity such as invitations and messages
  • Push notification tokens

How We Use Information

The information is used to:

  • Provide the core functionality of the app
  • Enable invitations and messaging between friends
  • Send notifications about invitations
  • Improve app performance and reliability

Third Party Services

This app uses third-party services including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Firestore

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

User Generated Content

Users may create invitations and communicate with friends. The app provides reporting and blocking mechanisms to help prevent abusive behavior.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data.
